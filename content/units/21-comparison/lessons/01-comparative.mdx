<Callout kind="remember" title="English first">
  You already use <strong>positive / comparative / superlative</strong> (*long, longer, longest*) and *than*. Latin *-ior* and *-issimus* (and irregulars) encode those degrees.
</Callout>

## Comparative

Regular: stem + **-ior** (m/f), **-ius** (n.)  
*longus* → *longior, longius*  
*fortis* → *fortior, fortius*

**Than:** *quam* + same case, or **ablative of comparison** (*mē fortior* — braver than I).
