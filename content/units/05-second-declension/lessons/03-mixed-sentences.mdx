## Mixing declensions

You can now build richer sentences:

| Word | Decl | Role example |
|------|------|----------------|
| puella | 1st | subject / object |
| servus | 2nd m. | subject / object |
| bellum | 2nd n. | subject or object (same form) |

<LatinExample latin="Servus aquam portat." english="The servant carries water." />
<LatinExample latin="Puella amīcum spectat." english="The girl looks at the friend." />
<LatinExample latin="Bellum in Italiā est." english="There is war in Italy. / War is in Italy." />
