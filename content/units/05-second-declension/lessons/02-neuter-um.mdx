## Neuter *-um* nouns

### Neuter law

In each number, **nominative = accusative** for neuters.

### bellum, bellī n. — war

| Case | Singular | Plural |
|------|----------|--------|
| Nom | bellum | bella |
| Gen | bellī | bellōrum |
| Dat | bellō | bellīs |
| Acc | bellum | bella |
| Abl | bellō | bellīs |

Also: <Latin>oppidum</Latin> town, <Latin>dōnum</Latin> gift, <Latin>verbum</Latin> word.
