## Vocabulary quiz: Mid-year

This is a **cumulative vocabulary quiz**—mixed words from earlier batches.

### How to approach
- Do not rush matching; wrong pairs fail the item.  
- Typing: macrons optional; use the main dictionary gloss.  
- If you miss items, use **More practice** until the standard is met.

  Mentally list any word families that always trip you (look-alikes, gender, verb principal parts).
</Callout>
