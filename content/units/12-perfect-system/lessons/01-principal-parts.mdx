<Callout kind="remember" title="English first">
  You already distinguished <strong>completed</strong> action (*has done*, *did*, *had done*, *will have done*) from ongoing past. The perfect system is Latin’s morphology for those meanings.
</Callout>

## Principal parts

Most verbs list **four** dictionary forms:

1. **1st person present** — *amō*  
2. **present infinitive** — *amāre*  
3. **1st person perfect** — *amāvī*  
4. **supine / perfect participle stem** — *amātum*

**Perfect stem** = 3rd part minus **-ī** → *amāv-*, *monu-*, *rēx-*, *cēp-*, *fu-*.
