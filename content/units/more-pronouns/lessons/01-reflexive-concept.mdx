## Reflexive meaning (English first)

A **reflexive** pronoun points back to the **subject**:

| Ordinary object | Reflexive |
|-----------------|-----------|
| Marcus sees **him** (someone else) | Marcus sees **himself** |
| They wash **them** (others) | They wash **themselves** |

Latin’s main 3rd-person reflexive is **sē** (also *sui, sibi, sē*).  
1st/2nd person reuse **mē / tē / nōs / vōs** as reflexives when the subject is I/you/we/you-all.
