## Interrogative *quis* (Year 1 intro)

| | M/F | N |
|--|-----|---|
| Nom | **quis** | **quid** |
| Acc | quem | **quid** |

Many other cases look like the **relative** (*cuius, cui, quō…*). Context:

- **Question:** *Quis est?* — Who is it?  
- **Relative:** *vir **quī** venit* — the man who comes  

Also common: *Ubi…? Cūr…? Quandō…?* (where, why, when)—not full pronoun charts, but question words you’ll meet in reading.
