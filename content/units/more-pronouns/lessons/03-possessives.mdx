## Possessive adjectives

These **agree** with the noun possessed (like *bonus*):

| | Meaning |
|--|---------|
| **meus -a -um** | my |
| **tuus -a -um** | your (sg.) |
| **suus -a -um** | his/her/its/their **own** |
| **noster -tra -trum** | our |
| **vester -tra -trum** | your (pl.) |

### suus vs eius

- *Marcus fīlium **suum** amat.* — his **own** son (Marcus is subject)  
- *Marcus fīlium **eius** amat.* — **his/her** son (someone else’s)

Year 1 goal: recognize and produce common forms; master the **suus / eius** distinction in reading.
