## Mixed pronoun practice

Year 1 toolkit:

| Type | Stars |
|------|--------|
| Personal | ego, tū, nōs, vōs; is ea id |
| Demonstrative | hic, ille |
| Relative | quī quae quod |
| Reflexive | sē (mē/tē…) |
| Possessive | meus, tuus, suus, noster, vester |
| Interrogative | quis, quid (intro) |

**Not full Year 1 load:** complete charts of *iste, ipse, īdem*, every interrogative form—those can deepen later.
