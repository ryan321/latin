## Verbs

A <KeyTerm>verb</KeyTerm> shows **action** or a **state of being**.

### Action vs being

| Kind | English examples |
|------|------------------|
| Action | carries, sees, loves, walks |
| Being / linking | is, are, was, seems |

### Person and number (preview)

Latin verbs care **who** is doing the action:

| Person | English idea |
|--------|----------------|
| 1st singular | I |
| 2nd singular | you (one person) |
| 3rd singular | he / she / it |
| 1st plural | we |
| 2nd plural | you (plural) |
| 3rd plural | they |

You will attach personal endings in the verb units. For now, notice the idea.

### *sum* — “I am” (first look)

Latin’s most common verb of being:

- <Latin>sum</Latin> — I am  
- <Latin>est</Latin> — he/she/it is  
- <Latin>sunt</Latin> — they are  

Full present chart comes with first declension sentences.

<LatinExample
  latin="Puella est in villā."
  english="The girl is in the country house."
  note="est = verb of being; Puella = noun (subject)."
