## Subjects and verbs agree

- Singular nominative subject → 3rd singular verb (**-t**)  
- Plural nominative subject → 3rd plural verb (**-nt**)

<LatinExample latin="Puella terram spectat." english="The girl looks at the land." />
<LatinExample latin="Puellae terram spectant." english="The girls look at the land." />

Latin word order is often **subject … object … verb**, but endings matter more than order.
