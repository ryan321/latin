## Objects, nōn, and questions

### Accusative objects

Action verbs often take a direct object in the **accusative**:

<LatinExample latin="Puella aquam portat." english="The girl carries water." />

### Negation

<Latin>nōn</Latin> = **not** (usually before the verb):

<LatinExample latin="Puella nōn spectat." english="The girl does not look / is not looking." />

### Questions with -ne

Attach **-ne** to the verb (or first word) for a simple yes/no question:

- <Latin>Amatne?</Latin> — Does he/she love?  
- <Latin>Portantne aquam?</Latin> — Are they carrying water?
