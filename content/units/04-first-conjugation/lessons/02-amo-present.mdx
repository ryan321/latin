## Amō — present active

First conjugation verbs have infinitives in **-āre** (dictionary: *amō, amāre…*).

### Pattern (amō)

| | Singular | Plural |
|--|----------|--------|
| 1 | amō | amāmus |
| 2 | amās | amātis |
| 3 | amat | amant |

### Other 1st-conjugation verbs

- <Latin>portō</Latin> — carry  
- <Latin>spectō</Latin> — look at / watch  
- <Latin>laudō</Latin> — praise  
- <Latin>vocō</Latin> — call  

Same personal endings; new stems.
