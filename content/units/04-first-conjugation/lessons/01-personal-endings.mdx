## Personal endings

A **finite** Latin verb form tells you **who** acts (person) and **how many** (number).

### Present active personal endings

| Person | Singular | Plural |
|--------|----------|--------|
| 1st | -ō | -mus |
| 2nd | -s | -tis |
| 3rd | -t | -nt |

You will attach these to verb stems (first conjugation next).

<Callout kind="remember">
  English needs “I / you / they” as separate words more often. Latin often builds that into the ending.
</Callout>
