## Vocabulary: Being & daily actions

**Theme:** sum + 1st conjugation verbs

Learn these words **actively**—matching, quizzes, and typing—not just reading the list once.

### Word list

| Latin | Dictionary info | English |
|-------|-----------------|---------|
| sum | esse, fuī | be, exist |
| amō | amāre, amāvī | love, like |
| portō | portāre | carry, bring |
| labōrō | labōrāre | work |
| habitō | habitāre | live, dwell |
| spectō | spectāre | watch, look at |
| vocō | vocāre | call |
| parō | parāre | prepare, get ready |
| servō | servāre | save, keep, guard |
| nāvigō | nāvigāre | sail |
| superō | superāre | overcome, surpass |
| nōn | indeclinable | not |

### How to study
1. Cover the English column; say the meaning.  
2. Cover the Latin; produce the Latin from English.  
3. Then do the drills below until the standard is met.
