## Passage reading (starter)

Read the whole passage first. Then answer—**from the Latin**, not from guessing.

### Strategy
1. Find each **verb** (*est*, *sunt*).  
2. Find each **nominative** subject.  
3. Notice *in* + ablative for **where**.
