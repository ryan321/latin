## Using cases

Latin shows the job of a noun with **endings**, not word order alone.

<LatinExample
  latin="Puella terram spectat."
  english="The girl looks at the land."
  note="puella = nominative subject · terram = accusative object"
/>

<LatinExample
  latin="Aqua est in silvā."
  english="Water is in the forest."
  note="in + ablative for place where"
/>
