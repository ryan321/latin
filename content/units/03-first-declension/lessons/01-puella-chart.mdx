## First declension

You already know what the cases **mean** (Unit 2). Now you learn how 1st-declension nouns **look** in each case.

Most first-declension nouns are **feminine** and end in **-a** in the nominative singular (dictionary form).

<KeyTerm>puella, puellae</KeyTerm> f. — girl  
(Dictionary form = nominative singular + genitive singular + gender.)

### Endings (learn these cold)

<EndingsStrip label="Singular" endings="a | ae | ae | am | ā" />
<EndingsStrip label="Plural" endings="ae | ārum | īs | ās | īs" />

### Case jobs (preview)

Cases show the **job** of the noun in the sentence — not English word order alone.

<CaseCards items="Nominative ~ Subject of the verb (the girl walks)|Genitive ~ Of / possession (the girl's water)|Dative ~ To / for (to the girl)|Accusative ~ Direct object (sees the girl)|Ablative ~ By / with / from; also in + place where (in the villa)" />

<LatinExample
