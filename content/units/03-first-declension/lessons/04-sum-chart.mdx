## Sum — present tense

<Latin>sum</Latin> = **I am**. The most common verb of being.

### Present indicative

| | Singular | Plural |
|--|----------|--------|
| 1 | sum | sumus |
| 2 | es | estis |
| 3 | est | sunt |

### With 1st-declension subjects

<LatinExample latin="Puella est in villā." english="The girl is in the country house." />
<LatinExample latin="Puellae sunt in silvā." english="The girls are in the forest." />

Subject of *sum* is **nominative**. Location after *in* is often **ablative**.
