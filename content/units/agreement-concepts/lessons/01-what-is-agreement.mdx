## What is agreement?

**Agreement** means: when words work together, they **match** on certain features.

English examples you already know:

- *this book* / *these books* (number)  
- *he walks* / *they walk* (person/number on the verb)

### Why Latin piles on more

Latin adjectives will match their noun in:

1. **Gender** (masculine / feminine / neuter)  
2. **Number** (singular / plural)  
3. **Case** (nominative, accusative, …)

That sounds like a lot—but the *idea* is the same as *these books*: the describing word **fits** the noun.

<Callout kind="remember">
  Master the idea of “fitting” first. Endings are just the Latin way to show the fit.
</Callout>
