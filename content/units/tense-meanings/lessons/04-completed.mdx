## Completed action

English perfect family:

| English | Idea | Latin name (later) |
|---------|------|---------------------|
| has/have walked; walked (completed) | finished action | **Perfect** |
| had walked | finished before another past | **Pluperfect** |
| will have walked | finished before a future point | **Future perfect** |

Stories often mix:

- **Imperfect** background: *were fighting*  
- **Perfect** plot step: *conquered*

Hold these English meanings firmly before Latin perfect endings.
