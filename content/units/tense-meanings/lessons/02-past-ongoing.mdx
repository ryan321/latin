## Past ongoing and habitual

English tools:

| English | Job |
|---------|-----|
| *was/were …ing* | ongoing in the past |
| *used to …* | past habit |
| *would …* (past habit) | repeated past |

**Simple past** *walked* can be a **completed** event: *She walked home (and arrived).*

### Latin names (coming soon)

- **Imperfect** ≈ was …ing / used to  
- **Perfect** ≈ has …ed / did (completed)—detailed soon
