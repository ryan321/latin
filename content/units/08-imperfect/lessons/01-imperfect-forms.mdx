## Imperfect tense

### Meaning

Often: **was/were …ing**, **used to**, past **ongoing** or **habitual** action.

### sum — imperfect

| | Singular | Plural |
|--|----------|--------|
| 1 | eram | erāmus |
| 2 | erās | erātis |
| 3 | erat | erant |

### Regular 1st/2nd conjugation

Present stem + **-bā-** + personal endings:

- <Latin>amābam, amābās, amābat…</Latin>  
- <Latin>monēbam, monēbās, monēbat…</Latin>  

(Note: 1st person singular ends in **-m**, not -ō, in the imperfect.)
