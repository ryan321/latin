<Callout kind="remember" title="English first">
  You already use English participles (*fighting*, *captured*) as describing words. Latin participles do the same jobs with their own forms.
</Callout>

## Present active participle

Form: present stem + **-ns** (*amāns, monēns, regēns, capiēns, audiēns*)  

Declines like a 3rd-declension adjective (*ingēns*): *amāns, amantis…*  

Meaning: **loving**, **while loving**, **who loves**.
