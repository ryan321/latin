## Agreement in phrases

### Attributive

Adjective + noun: <Latin>bona puella</Latin>, <Latin>magnus servus</Latin>, <Latin>parvum dōnum</Latin>.

### Predicate

With *sum*: <Latin>Puella est bona.</Latin> — The girl is good. (still agrees)

### Common adjectives

| Latin | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| bonus -a -um | good |
| malus -a -um | bad |
| magnus -a -um | large, great |
| parvus -a -um | small |
| multus -a -um | much, many |
