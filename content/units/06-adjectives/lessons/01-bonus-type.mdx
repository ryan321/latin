## bonus, bona, bonum

Adjectives like <Latin>bonus</Latin> (“good”) use **1st-declension endings for feminine** and **2nd-declension endings for masculine and neuter**.

### Agreement

An adjective agrees with its noun in:

1. **Gender**  
2. **Number**  
3. **Case**

It does **not** have to match the noun’s declension number: *magna silva*, *magnus servus*, *magnum bellum*.

### Dictionary form

Adjectives are listed with three nominative singulars: **bonus, bona, bonum** (m., f., n.).

<Callout kind="remember">
  The noun decides the gender; the adjective follows.
</Callout>
