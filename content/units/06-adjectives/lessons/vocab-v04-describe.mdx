## Vocabulary: Describing words

**Theme:** 1st/2nd adjectives

Learn these words **actively**—matching, quizzes, and typing—not just reading the list once.

### Word list

| Latin | Dictionary info | English |
|-------|-----------------|---------|
| bonus | bona, bonum | good |
| malus | mala, malum | bad, evil |
| magnus | magna, magnum | great, large |
| parvus | parva, parvum | small, little |
| multus | multa, multum | much, many |
| longus | longa, longum | long |
| altus | alta, altum | high, deep |
| novus | nova, novum | new |
| vērus | vēra, vērum | true |
| pulcher | pulchra, pulchrum | beautiful, handsome |

### How to study
1. Cover the English column; say the meaning.  
2. Cover the Latin; produce the Latin from English.  
3. Then do the drills below until the standard is met.
