### Where is Rome?

- On the **Italian** peninsula  
- By the **Tiber** River  
- In the central Mediterranean world Romans later called **Mare Nostrum** (“Our Sea”)

<MapCallout title="Italia">
  Picture a boot-shaped peninsula. Rome sits inland from the west coast, with access to the sea by river and roads that will later stitch the Empire together.
</MapCallout>

### Why this course pairs language and empire

Grammar is the engine. Context makes it human: family, army, forum, provinces — the world your sentences will describe.

<Callout kind="culture" title="Latium">
  Latin is named for <strong>Latium</strong>, the region of early Rome. As Rome grew, Latin grew with it.
</Callout>
