## Classical pronunciation (starter)

We use **classical** reconstruction (roughly the late Republic / early Empire), not ecclesiastical. Getting the sounds right early makes reading aloud and listening later much easier.

<Callout kind="remember">
  In this app, **macrons are optional** when you type answers — unless a lesson is specifically about vowel quantity.
</Callout>

### Stress (accent)

Stress usually falls on the **second-to-last** syllable if that syllable is long; otherwise on the **third-to-last**. You will practice this with real words soon.
