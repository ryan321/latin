## Syllables & stress

Latin words are divided into **syllables**. Stress (accent) usually follows a clear rule.

### Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Ultima** | Last syllable |
| **Penult** | Second-to-last |
| **Antepenult** | Third-to-last |

### The rule

1. Look at the **penult**.
2. If it is **long**, stress it.
3. If it is **short**, stress the **antepenult**.

A syllable is long if it has a long vowel/diphthong, or a short vowel followed by two (or more) consonants (simplified starter rule).
