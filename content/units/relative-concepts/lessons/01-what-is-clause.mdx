## Relative clauses in English

A **relative clause** is a mini-sentence that **describes a noun**:

- The soldier **who won** is brave.  
- The city **that fell** was large.  
- The laws **which the people hear** are old.

Common relative words: **who, which, that, whom, whose**.

Latin will use **quī, quae, quod** for the same job—after you own the English idea.
