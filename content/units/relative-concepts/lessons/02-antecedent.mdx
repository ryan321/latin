## The antecedent

The **antecedent** is the noun (or pronoun) the relative clause talks about.

- The **soldier** who won… → antecedent = *soldier*  
- The **laws** that we keep… → antecedent = *laws*

### Two jobs of the relative word

1. **Points back** to the antecedent (who/which = that person/thing)  
2. **Has a job inside its own clause** (subject, object, …)

Example: *the man **whom** I see*  
- antecedent = *man*  
- *whom* = object of *see*

Latin keeps both jobs—gender/number from the antecedent, **case** from the job inside the clause.
