## Than and comparison

Comparatives often use **than**:

- *This mountain is **higher than** that one.*

Superlatives often use **of/in** a group:

- *the bravest **of** the soldiers*

English irregulars (you already know): *good / better / best*, *bad / worse / worst*, *much / more / most*.

Latin has irregulars too (*bonus / melior / optimus*)—same **meaning slots**.
