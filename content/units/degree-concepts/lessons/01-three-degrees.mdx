## Three degrees

Adjectives (and adverbs) come in **degrees**:

| Degree | English tools | Example |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| **Positive** | base form | long, brave, careful |
| **Comparative** | *-er* / *more* | longer, braver, more careful |
| **Superlative** | *-est* / *most* / *very* | longest, bravest, most careful |

Superlative can mean **the most** or **very** (*most beautiful* / *very beautiful*).

Latin will use endings like **-ior** and **-issimus** for these same ideas.
