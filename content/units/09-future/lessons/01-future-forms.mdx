<Callout kind="remember" title="English first">
  You already practice <strong>future time</strong> with *will* / *going to*. Latin future endings carry the same idea without a separate helper word.
</Callout>

## Future tense

### Meaning
**will / shall** (simple future).

### sum — future
| | Singular | Plural |
|--|----------|--------|
| 1 | erō | erimus |
| 2 | eris | eritis |
| 3 | erit | erunt |

### 1st / 2nd conjugation
Present stem + **-bō, -bis, -bit, -bimus, -bitis, -bunt**:

- <Latin>amābō, amābis, amābit…</Latin>
- <Latin>monēbō, monēbis, monēbit…</Latin>

**Do not confuse** with imperfect **-bam, -bās, -bat…**
