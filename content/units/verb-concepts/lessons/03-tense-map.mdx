## What is tense?

**Tense** is the verb’s system for **time** (and often **aspect**: ongoing vs completed).

You already use tense in English:

| Idea | English examples |
|------|------------------|
| Present | walks, is walking |
| Past (simple) | walked, fought |
| Past (ongoing / habitual) | was walking, used to walk |
| Future | will walk |
| Completed | has walked, had walked, will have walked |

### Year 1 preview (names you will meet in Latin)

- **Present** — happening now / general truth  
- **Imperfect** — was …ing / used to  
- **Future** — will  
- **Perfect** — has …ed / did (completed)  
- **Pluperfect** — had …ed  
- **Future perfect** — will have …ed  

You do **not** need Latin forms yet—only clear English labels. A later unit drills these meanings hard before imperfect and perfect Latin.
