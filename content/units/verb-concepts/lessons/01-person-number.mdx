## Person and number

A finite verb answers: **who** is involved, and **how many**?

### Person

| Person | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **1st** | the speaker(s): *I / we* |
| **2nd** | the one(s) spoken to: *you* |
| **3rd** | someone/something else: *he, she, it, they*, or a noun (*the girl*) |

### Number

| Number | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Singular** | one |
| **Plural** | more than one |

English often shows this with a **pronoun** (*I walk* / *they walk*).  
Latin will often show it with a **verb ending**—same idea, different tool.

<Callout kind="remember">
  Before you learn Latin endings, you must instantly know: is this I, you, he/she, we, you-all, or they?
</Callout>
