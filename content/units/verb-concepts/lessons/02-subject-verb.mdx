## Subject and verb

The **subject** is who or what the sentence is about—usually the doer with an action verb.

- *The **girl** carries water.* → subject = *girl*  
- ***They** love Rome.* → subject = *they*

### Agreement

The verb must **fit** the subject:

- *The girl **carries*** (singular)  
- *The girls **carry*** (plural)

Latin will enforce the same rule with endings: a 3rd-person plural subject pairs with a 3rd-person plural verb ending.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Find them first">
  When reading any language: find the <strong>verb</strong>, then ask who/what it is about (the subject).
</Callout>
