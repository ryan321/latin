## Agent and “by…”

In a passive sentence, the **doer** may appear in a **by-phrase**:

- *The city was captured **by the Romans**.* → agent = Romans  

Sometimes the doer is omitted: *The city was captured.* (by someone)

### Agent vs means

- **Agent (person):** *by the soldier*  
- **Means (thing):** *with a sword / by fire* (instrument)

Latin keeps this distinction. Know it in English first.
