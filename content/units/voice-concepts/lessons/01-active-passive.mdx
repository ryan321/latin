## Active vs passive

**Voice** is about the relationship between the **subject** and the **action**.

| Voice | Subject’s role | English example |
|-------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Active** | does the action | *The soldier **captures** the city.* |
| **Passive** | receives the action | *The city **is captured**.* |

English passive often uses a form of *be* + past participle: *is captured, was praised, will be sent*.

You have been using **active** Latin verbs (*amat, dūcit*). Passive Latin will flip the focus—**after** this concept is solid.
