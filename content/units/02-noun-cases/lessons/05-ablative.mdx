## Ablative — by, with, from; place where

The <KeyTerm>ablative</KeyTerm> is a multi-tool case. For Year 1 starters, learn these senses:

### Core starter meanings

| Idea | English | Note |
|------|---------|------|
| **By / with / from** | with a sword; from the road | Means, separation (details later) |
| **Place where** | **in** the forest; **on** the table | Especially *in* + ablative |

### *in* + ablative (high priority)

- in the villa → *in villā*  
- in the forest → *in silvā*  

The preposition *in* with ablative = location (**where?**).  
(Later you will also meet *in* + accusative = motion into.)

### Mini Latin

<LatinExample
  latin="Aqua est in silvā."
  english="Water is in the forest."
  note="silvā = ablative after in (place where)."
/>

<Callout kind="remember">
  When you see <Latin>in</Latin> + a noun in early lessons, ask: <strong>where?</strong> → ablative.
</Callout>
