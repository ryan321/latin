## Genitive & dative

### Genitive — of / possession

The <KeyTerm>genitive</KeyTerm> often answers **whose?** or **of what?**

- the water **of the girl**  
- the **girl’s** water  
- the road **of Rome** (Rome’s road)

In Latin dictionaries, nouns are listed with nominative and genitive (e.g. *puella, puellae*) so you know the stem and declension.

### Dative — to / for

The <KeyTerm>dative</KeyTerm> often marks the **indirect object**: to whom / for whom something is given, shown, or said.

- She gives water **to the girl**.  
- He prepares a gift **for the friend**.
