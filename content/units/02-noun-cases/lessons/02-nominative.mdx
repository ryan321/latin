## Nominative — the subject

The <KeyTerm>nominative</KeyTerm> case marks the **subject**: who or what is doing the action, or who/what **is** something.

### English sense

- **The girl** walks.  
- **Water** is cold.  
- **Rome** is great.  

Ask: *Who/what is the sentence about as the doer or topic of “is”?* → subject → nominative in Latin.

### With a linking verb

With *sum / est / sunt* (“am / is / are”), the subject is nominative — and so is a **predicate noun** renaming the subject:

- *Puella est*… → the girl is…  
- Later: *Puella est agricola* patterns (when you have more vocab).
