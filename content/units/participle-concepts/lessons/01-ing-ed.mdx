## Participles in English

A **participle** is a verb form that often works like an **adjective**.

| Type | English | Rough meaning |
|------|---------|----------------|
| Present | *loving, fighting, running* | ongoing / who is …ing |
| Past | *loved, captured, broken, written* | often passive or completed |

Examples:

- the **running** boy  
- the **captured** city  
- **Warned**, he left.  

Latin has participles too (present active *amāns*; perfect passive *amātus*). Learn the **English jobs** first.
