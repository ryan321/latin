## Participial phrases

Participles often drag objects or modifiers with them:

- *the girl **carrying water***  
- *the city **captured by the army***  
- ***Having been warned**, the soldier fled.*

You can often expand them:

- *carrying water* → *who is carrying water*  
- *captured by the army* → *that was captured by the army*

Latin ablative absolutes and agreeing participles will feel less alien if you can already expand English phrases into clauses.
