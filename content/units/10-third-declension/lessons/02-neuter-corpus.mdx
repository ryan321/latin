## Third declension — neuter

Example: **corpus, corporis n.** — body.

| | Singular | Plural |
|--|----------|--------|
| Nom | corpus | corpora |
| Gen | corporis | corporum |
| Dat | corporī | corporibus |
| Acc | corpus | corpora |
| Abl | corpore | corporibus |

Also: *nōmen, nōminis*; *flūmen, flūminis*; *tempus, temporis*; *caput, capitis*.
