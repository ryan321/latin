## i-stems (Year 1 intro)

Many 3rd-declension nouns (and adjectives) are **i-stems**.

**Watch for:**
- Genitive plural **-ium** (urbium, hostium, cīvium)
- Ablative singular sometimes **-ī** (especially neuters like *marī*)
- Neuter plural **-ia** (*maria*, *animālia*)

Examples: *urbs, urbis*; *hostis, hostis*; *cīvis, cīvis*; *mare, maris*; *animal, animālis*; *nox, noctis*; *pars, partis*.
