## Third declension (m/f)

Dictionary form: **rēx, rēgis m.** — king.

**Stem** = genitive without **-is** → **rēg-**

| | Singular | Plural |
|--|----------|--------|
| Nom | rēx | rēgēs |
| Gen | rēgis | rēgum |
| Dat | rēgī | rēgibus |
| Acc | rēgem | rēgēs |
| Abl | rēge | rēgibus |

Common lemmas: *mīles, mīlitis*; *pater, patris*; *māter, mātris*; *dux, ducis*; *urbs, urbis*; *lēx, lēgis*; *vōx, vōcis*.
