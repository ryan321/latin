## Vocabulary: Warn, see, have

**Theme:** 2nd conjugation & related

Learn these words **actively**—matching, quizzes, and typing—not just reading the list once.

| videō | vidēre, vīdī | see |

| timeō | timēre | fear |
| teneō | tenēre, tenuī | hold, keep |

| respondeō | respondēre | answer, reply |
